The practice

"A building should earn its keep twice — once for the people who commission it, and once for the people who inherit it."

MD Mojammel Khan Paris is a B.Arch student at GSTU, expecting to graduate in 2026, and an incoming M.Arch candidate. This portfolio collects academic and thesis work developed across the program.

The work here is grounded in daylight, material honesty, and the conditions already present on site — with a particular interest in civic and educational architecture, including the elementary school design in Gopalganj featured above.
